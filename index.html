Phase 1 · The Core

Everything a college actually runs on.

No AI gimmicks yet — just the six things every student, teacher and admin opens every single day.

01

Attendance

Teachers mark it, students track it, admins get the monthly report — no more shouting roll numbers across a hall.

02

Assignments & notes

Upload, submit, grade. PDFs, PPTs, ZIPs and video links, all in one place.

03

Results & marks

Internal marks, GPA and CGPA calculated automatically, exportable as a PDF.

04

Timetable

Daily, weekly, department-wise and semester-wise — always the current version.

05

Notices

One post reaches every student and teacher it's meant for. No forwarding required.

06

Role-based access

Admin, teacher and student each see exactly what they need — and nothing they don't.
